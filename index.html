How to use 31-Day Reset

Your daily routine

Every day has two tasks: a workout and an evening habit, at the times you chose. Tap a task to read exactly what to do, then tap Mark done.

Guided workouts

Tap Start today's workout. The app shows one move at a time with a countdown, rest timers and a voice coach, and keeps your screen on. Each move shows how to do it, plus an easier and a harder version. On test days it saves your results for you. When you finish, the workout is ticked off automatically.

Your if-then plan

Decide where you will train and what you will do when life gets in the way. Edit it any time from the plan card or Settings.

Badges and certificate

Earn badges for your first day and at 7, 14 and 21 full days. Finish Day 31 to unlock a personalised certificate you can save and share.

Exercise pictures

In a guided workout, a moving figure shows each exercise from start to finish. During rests it shows the next move so you can get ready.

Your progress

Charts show your sleep for every day and how your measurements changed from Day 1 to Day 31. Tap a point or bar to see its value.

Workout buddy

Invite a friend on WhatsApp and send each other a quick update every day. People who train with someone are more likely to keep going.

Round 2

After Day 31 you can start Round 2: the same plan, one level up, with your Round 1 results kept in your history.

Colours

Red is strength, orange is cardio, green is mobility and recovery, gold is your final test. Evening habits have their own colours: sleep, water, food and stress.

Moving between days

Use the arrows or tap any day in the grid. The dot button takes you back to today.

Check-in days

On Day 1, 14, 15, 27, 30 and 31 a gold note tells you what to record and takes you straight there.

Reminders

Calendar alerts are the most reliable: they ring at your set times even when the app is closed. App notifications are an extra nudge.

Why can't I change the exercises?

The plan gets a little harder each week and covers your whole body over 31 days. Following it in order is what gets results.

Missed a day? Catch-up days

Life happens. Each week you get one catch-up day: if you miss a day, it is covered automatically and your streak stays alive. It cannot cover two misses in a row, so never miss twice. You can also go back and complete a missed day's tasks later. If your streak does reset, just start again: your best streak is always saved.

Your data

Everything stays on your own phone. Use Save backup file now and then.

Safety

Stop if you feel chest pain, dizziness or unusual breathlessness. Check with a doctor first if you have a health condition.
