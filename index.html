How to use 31-Day Reset

Your daily routine

Every day has two tasks: a workout and an evening habit, at the times you chose. Open the app, tap a task to read exactly what to do, then tap Mark done.

Moving between days

Use the arrows to go back or ahead, or tap any number in the 31-day grid. Green means both tasks are done, amber means one. The dot button takes you back to today.

Sleep

Each morning, enter how many hours you slept. The app shows your average.

Check-in days

On Day 1, 14, 15, 27, 30 and 31 a red note tells you what to record and takes you straight to the right box.

Reminders

Calendar alerts are the most reliable: they ring at your set times even when the app is closed. App notifications are an extra nudge.

Missed a day?

No problem. Carry on with the next day. Never miss two in a row.

Your data

Everything stays on your own phone. Use Save backup file now and then so you never lose it.

Safety

Stop if you feel chest pain, dizziness or unusual breathlessness. Check with a doctor first if you have a health condition.
