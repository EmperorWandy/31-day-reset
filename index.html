How to use 31-Day Reset

Your daily routine

Every day has two tasks: a workout and an evening habit, at the times you chose. Tap a task to read exactly what to do, then tap Mark done.

Colours

Red is strength, orange is cardio, green is mobility and recovery, gold is your final test. Evening habits have their own colours: sleep, water, food and stress.

Moving between days

Use the arrows or tap any day in the grid. The dot button takes you back to today.

Check-in days

On Day 1, 14, 15, 27, 30 and 31 a gold note tells you what to record and takes you straight there.

Reminders

Calendar alerts are the most reliable: they ring at your set times even when the app is closed. App notifications are an extra nudge.

Why can't I change the exercises?

The plan gets a little harder each week and covers your whole body over 31 days. Following it in order is what gets results.

Missed a day?

Carry on with the next day. Never miss two in a row.

Your data

Everything stays on your own phone. Use Save backup file now and then.

Safety

Stop if you feel chest pain, dizziness or unusual breathlessness. Check with a doctor first if you have a health condition.
