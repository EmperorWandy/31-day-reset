Privacy policy

31-Day Reset, by Akinwande Akinkunmi Adeniyi. Last updated 3 November 2026.

31-Day Reset is a fitness and wellness coach that guides you through a 31-day plan. This page explains what information the app uses and where it goes. In short: everything you enter stays on your own phone. There are no accounts, no adverts and no tracking.

What the app stores, and where

The app saves the following only in your phone's own browser storage:

  • your first name, chosen goal, start date, workout time, evening habit time and if-then plan
  • the tasks you tick off, your streaks and badges
  • measurements you choose to record, such as weight, waist, resting pulse, test results and hours of sleep
  • your workout log, including minutes, effort rating and, if you switched on GPS, the distance covered
  • your settings, such as the coach voice

None of this is sent to the author or to anyone else. The author cannot see your progress.

Location (GPS)

The app asks for your location only if you tick "Measure my distance with GPS" before an outdoor walk or run. It reads your position while the workout screen is open, works out the distance on your phone and saves only the total distance. Your route and positions are not stored or sent anywhere. You can say no, or switch location off in your phone's settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows reminders for your workout and evening habit. These are created on your phone. No notification service receives your data.

Things that only happen when you tap them

  • Google Calendar or calendar file: if you choose to add your plan to a calendar, the event details are passed to the calendar app you pick.
  • WhatsApp sharing: if you share a badge or invite a workout buddy, the message you see is passed to WhatsApp.
  • Backup file: if you save a backup, a file is saved to your phone. You decide where it goes.

Services the app loads

The app and its coach voice recordings are hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services receive your device's IP address and basic browser details when they deliver the files. They do not receive your fitness data. See the GitHub privacy statement and the Google privacy policy.

Children

The app is meant for adults aged 18 and over. It is not designed for children.

Deleting your data

Tap Start over in the app's Settings to erase everything, or uninstall the app. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, this removes all of your data.

Health notice

The app gives general fitness and wellness guidance. It is not medical advice. Speak to a doctor before starting if you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, diabetes, a recent operation or injury, or are pregnant.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. For questions, email akinkunmiakinwande@gmail.com.
