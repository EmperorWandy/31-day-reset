Privacy policy

31-Day Reset, by Akinwande Akinkunmi Adeniyi. Last updated 14 November 2026 (version 7.2).

31-Day Reset is a fitness and wellness coach that guides you through a 31-day plan. This page explains what information the app uses and where it goes. In short: everything you enter stays on your own phone, unless you choose to copy it to your own Google Drive or share workouts with Health Connect. There are no 31-Day Reset accounts, no adverts and no tracking.

What the app stores, and where

The app saves the following only in your phone's own browser storage:

  • your first name, chosen goal, start date, workout time, evening habit time and if-then plan
  • the tasks you tick off, your streaks and badges
  • measurements you choose to record, such as weight, waist, resting pulse, test results and hours of sleep
  • your workout log, including minutes, effort rating and, if you switched on GPS, the distance covered
  • your food and water log: the foods you tap or type for each meal and the number of glasses of water
  • your settings, such as the coach voice and light or dark look

None of this is sent to the author or to anyone else. The author cannot see your progress.

Location (GPS)

The app asks for your location only if you tick "Measure my distance with GPS" before an outdoor walk or run. It reads your position while the workout screen is open, works out the distance on your phone and saves only the total distance. Your route and positions are not stored or sent anywhere. You can say no, or switch location off in your phone's settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.

Step counting (Android app)

If you allow "physical activity" access in the Android app, it reads your phone's built-in step sensor a few times a day to work out your daily steps. Only the daily totals are kept, on your phone, for the last 40 days. They are shown in the app and on the home screen widget and are never sent anywhere. You can turn this off in your phone's app settings at any time.

Health Connect (Android app)

If you tap Connect to Health Connect and allow it, the Android app adds each guided workout you finish to Health Connect on your phone: the start and end time, the type of workout, its title (for example "Day 4: Lower body") and, if GPS was on, the distance. This lets the workout appear in other health apps you have allowed to use Health Connect, such as Samsung Health, Google Fit or Fitbit.

  • 31-Day Reset only writes exercise sessions and distance. It does not read any data from Health Connect.
  • The data goes to Health Connect on your phone, not to the author or any server run by the app.
  • You can stop this at any time in Health Connect's settings, and delete the workouts there.

Data written to Health Connect is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used for anything other than showing your workouts in the health apps you choose.

Google Drive backup

If you tap Connect Google Drive and sign in with Google, the app saves one file, "31-Day Reset backup.json", in your own Google Drive. It holds the same information listed above, so you can bring your progress back on a new phone.

  • The app asks only for permission to see and change files it created itself. It cannot see any other files in your Google Drive.
  • The file is stored by Google in your account. The author cannot see it.
  • While the app is open and connected, it updates the file after your changes. It does not run in the background for this.
  • Tap Disconnect to stop. The file stays in your Google Drive until you delete it there.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows reminders for your workout and evening habit. In the Android app these are set as alarms on your phone, so they still arrive when the app is closed and after a restart. They are created on your phone and no notification service receives your data.

Things that only happen when you tap them

  • Google Calendar or calendar file: if you choose to add your plan to a calendar, the event details are passed to the calendar app you pick.
  • WhatsApp sharing: if you share a badge or invite a workout buddy, the message you see is passed to WhatsApp.
  • Backup file: if you save a backup, a file is saved to your phone. You decide where it goes.
  • Google sign-in: if you use Google Drive backup, the app loads Google's sign-in service, and Google handles your sign-in under its own privacy policy.

Services the app loads

The app and its coach voice recordings are hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services receive your device's IP address and basic browser details when they deliver the files. They do not receive your fitness data. See the GitHub privacy statement and the Google privacy policy.

Children

The app is meant for adults aged 18 and over. It is not designed for children.

Deleting your data

Tap Start over in the app's Settings to erase everything on your phone, or uninstall the app. If you used Google Drive backup, also delete "31-Day Reset backup.json" from your Google Drive. If you used Health Connect, you can delete the workouts in Health Connect's settings.

Health notice

The app gives general fitness and wellness guidance. It is not medical advice. Speak to a doctor before starting if you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, diabetes, a recent operation or injury, or are pregnant.
